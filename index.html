Caring for Baby Elephants

Montano Leader Habit

Habit 1: Be Proactive & Habit 4: Think Win-Win

CNB Competency (Guatemala ESL Standards): Reads aloud and silently with grade-appropriate speed, accuracy, and comprehension to extract factual details and main ideas from varied texts.

Learning Outcome: Identifies explicit details, sequence of events, and vocabulary contextually from the reading text.

Key Skills: Critical thinking, active communication, team collaboration, empathy, decision making.

Evaluation Stages: Diagnostic (Introductory brainstorm), Formative (Individual guide checks & group active monitoring), Summative (Plenary presentation & rubric evaluation).

Resources Needed: Student Book / Workbook, notebooks, digital projector, chart paper/markers.

I

Warm-Up & Brain Activation

Connecting prior knowledge & stimulating curiosity

Time: 5 Minutes

Warm-Up Activity / Brain Gym

Quick Brainstorming: Show an image of a baby elephant. Ask students: "What do baby animals need to survive when they don't have parents?" Write 3 key ideas on the board.

Key Guiding Question

"Who helps animals when they are lost in the wild, and how can humans care for them?"

Montano Leader Habit in Action:

Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood: Listen respectfully to your classmates' answers during the opening discussion without interrupting.

II

Individual Work & Reading Comprehension

Autonomous reading, task execution, and self-evaluation

Time: 20 Minutes

Instructions for Students:

Read the text carefully in your book. Complete the True/False exercise and put the story events in order.

Book Reference: Student Book Pages 20-21
III

Cooperative Teamwork

Structured group task with assigned roles & concrete output

Time: 30 Minutes

Leader

Keeps team on task & manages time

Writer

Writes group summary / poster

Speaker

Presents solution during plenary

Researcher

Checks book facts & details

Group Collaborative Challenge

In pairs or trios, create a "Care Plan Poster" for a baby elephant. Detail 3 main needs mentioned in the text (e.g., milk, new family/trust, safety) and explain why each is important using full sentences.

Montano Habit Strategy: "Synergize (Together is Better)"
Product: Care Poster / Graphic Organizer
IV

Plenary Presentation & Interactive Review

Team sharing, targeted questions, and vocabulary game

Time: 15 Minutes
